# Breakout board: placement

This is the note for plan Task 14, Step 3. It says where `breakout.kicad_pcb` puts things
and which of those positions no source decides. The board file is generated by
`hardware/gen/gen_breakout_pcb.py` and checked by `hardware/gen/check_breakout_placement.py`.
It has every footprint placed and every pad on its net. It has no track, via or pour:
routing is a person's, in pcbnew. Nothing here was measured on hardware.

It follows `floorplan.md` and `constraints.md`, and cites both by section and ID.

A placement the sources do not decide is marked **[engineering judgment]** with its reason.
Where the sources lack a dimension the text says "not in the sources".

**Do not edit the board file's placement by hand, and do not re-run the generator once the
board is routed.** The generator writes the file whole. It refuses to overwrite a board
that has routed items unless it is given `--force`.

## 1. The frame

The board file uses KiCad's frame: x to the right, y down. `floorplan.md` uses X to the
right and Y from the front edge (0) to the back edge (240). The two agree on x, and

    board y = 240 − floorplan Y.

So pcbnew shows the board as `floorplan.md` §7 draws it: from above, back edge at the top,
nothing mirrored. Every coordinate below is given as (x, y) in the board file's frame.

## 4. The star

`J1` is at (173.5, 80) on the `AGND` side of the split, its pins running down to
(173.5, 90.16). `NT1` is on the split at (170, 94.5).

- **`J1` is beside the line, not on it. [engineering judgment]** Its two ground pins are
  both `AGND` (G-15) and pins 1 and 2 are the ±12 V feeds, so four of its five pins belong
  on the `AGND` side. A through-hole pad on the line would also pierce the L2 and L3 gap.
- **Two nets therefore cross the split at the inlet:** `P5_RAW` (`J1` pin 3 to `F4`) and
  `FAN_12V_RAW` (`F1` to `D44`), both within 20 mm of `NT1`. G-11 allows them as amended
  on 2026-11-20 (ruling R45), and the check declares both.
- **`U1`, `D3`, `D44` and fan header `J52` are under the IO Board**, at x < 160.
  **[engineering judgment]**: P-13 wants `U1` at the `+5V` entry, and the entry is at the
  star. X-3's height limit is met, since all four are low parts under a board at least
  29 mm up (floorplan §6.6). But `J52`'s fan lead plugs in under the IO Board, and `U1`,
  a heat source of unbudgeted size (floorplan §5.2), sits in the air the CM5 exhausts.
- `F1`, `F2`, `F3`, `D1`, `D2` and the ±12 V entry capacitors are on the `AGND` side;
  `F4`, `D3`, `D44`, `U1`, `NT2`, `NT3` and the `+5V` entry capacitors on the `DGND` side

## 7. Everything else

The other 413 parts are placed by rule, not by hand. For each the generator finds the part
it serves, then takes the nearest free position to the pads it connects to, inside its own
domain's region. The rules, in order:

1. a table of overrides, where a constraint asks for something the netlist's own rule
   would not give: K-7, K-19, K-21, K-26, P-7, P-9, G-14;
2. a clamp diode goes with the series resistor on its node (K-14, K-28);
3. a part on a net that reaches a panel connector goes at that connector (K-14, K-15,
   K-28, K-36), unless it is a pull-up;
4. a part on a signal net goes with the IC on its least-shared net;
5. a bypass capacitor goes to an IC on its own sheet that uses its rail, one per IC in
   reference order (K-35).

**[engineering judgment]** for rules 4 and 5, and for every position they produce. They are
proposals. A person who wants a part elsewhere moves it in the generator, by adding it to
`ANCHORS`, before routing starts.

Search parameters, none of which is a dimension of the design: 0.25 mm between courtyards,
1.0 mm from a courtyard to a board edge, a 0.25 mm grid. Reference text is 0.8 mm high and
horizontal, and each has its own free box.

- **Minimum track widths are rules, not only class defaults.** KiCad draws a net class's
  width and does not enforce it. `breakout.kicad_dru` has one `track_width` minimum per
  class (N-1 to N-7), a 0.2 mm floor for every other net (N-8), and 0.15 mm inside the
  courtyards of `U55`, `U3` and `U4` only (S-5). Tried on a scratch copy: a 0.2 mm track
  on `P5_RAW`, a 0.15 mm track on a default net and a 0.2 mm track on `A_PD1` each fail
  DRC as `track_width`; the same nets at 1.0, 0.2 and 0.25 mm, and a 0.15 mm track inside
  `U55`'s courtyard, pass.
- **N-13 is applied to all 129 nets that reach a panel connector** (as amended 2026-11-20,
  ruling R45). 30 of them have no series resistor: the 24 optocoupler outputs to `J45`,
  `RWD_BTN`, `AO_GND_TPC`, and `U11`'s four outputs to `J3`. The one exemption is between
  `U11`'s own pads, 0.2 mm apart at 0.65 mm pitch. Whether `U11`'s outputs should have
  series resistors at all is K-32 and constraints Open question 9.
- **S-5's 0.15 mm is forced by three packages, not one.** `U55`, and `U3` and `U4`
  (HVSSOP-8, 0.5 mm pads at 0.65 mm pitch). Each has a rule covering its own pads only.
  `constraints.md` S-5 now names all three.
- `gen_breakout.py` and the sheet generators rewrite `breakout.kicad_pro` as a stub.
  Re-run `gen_breakout_pcb.py`'s project step after them; the check fails if the classes
  are missing.

With `--schematic-parity` there are 30 warnings and no error: 18 ICs whose footprint has
no Description field, and 12 stock footprints (net ties, test points, the earth stud) that
the library marks "exclude from BOM" while the schematic lists them. No net, reference,
value, footprint or DNP flag differs.
